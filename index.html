    Good to know

    Questions,
    answered

    Still curious? Send us a message and we will happily help.

    Do I need any floral experience?

    Not at all. Every workshop is beginner friendly. We guide you through simple bouquet balance and color pairing step by step.

    What is included in the price?

    All flowers, greenery, wrapping and tools are included. You only need to bring yourself and a little curiosity.

    How many people can I book for?

    You can reserve one to four seats per booking. Each session welcomes up to 12 guests so everyone gets personal attention.

    How long does a workshop last?

    Every session lasts two hours, including a short introduction to the season’s flowers.

    Can I take my arrangement home?

    Yes. Your finished arrangement is yours to keep as a small memory of the season.

    Is this a real booking?

    Not yet. LunaFlora is a concept website for academic demonstration, so dates, prices and contact details are demo data and nothing is stored or charged.
